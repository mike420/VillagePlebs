[size=6][b]VillagePlebs[/b][/size]

A viking companion, a follower NPC who plays like a player: comes on your adventures, fights beside you, lives its own life at home, and gets better as it goes.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Press [b]J[/b] to summon your companion or open its menu ([b]E[/b] on it too). Hold [b]J[/b]: your companions come with you, or go home.
[*][b]Point and command:[/b] aim at something and press [b]H[/b]: an enemy (they attack it), a tree, rock or plant (it works it), your chest (it puts its things in), a cart (it pulls it), the ground (it waits there), the sky (it comes back).
[*][b]Let it decide:[/b] it reads what you are doing. It fights beside you, mines or chops beside you and keeps close while you explore; where a place is too dangerous for its gear it tells you and only defends.
[*][b]Combat:[/b] timed parries (it learns each creature's swing), dodge rolls, flanking, backstabs, weapon specials, meads, kiting with a bow, and care around bosses.
[*][b]Follows you anywhere:[/b] through portals, into crypts and caves, onto boats and through doors; it steers round stakes and fires and gets itself unstuck.
[*][b]Home life:[/b] it takes a free bed and empty chests, works towards better gear (gathering, smelting, crafting and upgrading at your stations), hunts, cooks, keeps its fires burning and repairs its gear and your base. While you are away it catches up on all of it.
[*][b]Needs like a player:[/b] food, stamina, skills that rise, carry weight, weather and rest.
[*][b]Gear slots of its own[/b]: give and take its gear and what is in its bag from its menu.
[*][b]A journal[/b] of its life, small talk that fits the moment, and a menu with its status, stats, orders, home, gear, looks and companions.
[*][b]EpicMMO System:[/b] it earns experience and levels from what it kills and gathers.
[*][b]StarLevelSystem:[/b] it leaves companions alone, as it does players: no copies, no star levels or modifiers, and it never deletes one.
[*][b]Languages:[/b] English, Russian, German, Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, Polish and Turkish, following the game's language setting.
[/list]


[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Everyone in the world needs the mod, [b]the host too[/b]: without it the game deletes saved companions. Each companion belongs to whoever summoned it. The host decides how many companions each player can have, what they do while you're away, respawn time, base health and stamina, when they eat, starting skill and EpicMMO experience.

Restart the game after installing or updating so the companion registers cleanly.


[size=5][b]Configuration[/b][/size]

Settings are in [b]BepInEx/config/MagicMike.VillagePlebs.cfg[/b]. Change them in game in the [b]Mod Settings[/b] panel ([b]F7[/b]), or in r2modman/Gale/[url=https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Azumatt/Official_BepInEx_ConfigurationManager/]Configuration Manager[/url]. Fight Range and Show Decisions are also in the companion's menu. The settings marked synced are the server's in multiplayer.
