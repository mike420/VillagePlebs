[size=6][b]VillagePlebs[/b][/size]

Create multiple viking companions, follower NPCs who play like a player: they come on your adventures, fight beside you, live their own lives at home, and get better as they go.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Press [b]J[/b] to summon your companion or open its menu ([b]E[/b] on it too). Hold [b]J[/b]: your companions come with you, or go home. A new companion gets straight to work on its own mission (making or upgrading its gear) where you summoned it, until you tell it to come with you.
[*][b]Point and command:[/b] aim at something and press [b]H[/b]: an enemy (it attacks it), a tree, rock or plant (it works it), your chest (it puts its things in), a cart (it pulls it), its tombstone (it fetches its things), a free bed (it makes it its bed), the ground (it waits there), the sky (it comes back). It only takes an order it has the tools for, in its bag or its own chests (it fetches them first), and your orders come before its mission; jobs it has no tool for wait while it works on its mission.
[*][b]Let it decide:[/b] it reads what you are doing. It fights beside you, mines or chops beside you and keeps close while you explore; where a place is too dangerous for its gear it tells you and only defends.
[*][b]Combat:[/b] timed parries (it learns each creature's swing), dodge rolls, flanking, backstabs, weapon specials, meads, kiting with a bow, and care around bosses.
[*][b]Follows you anywhere:[/b] through portals, into crypts and caves, onto boats and through doors; it steers round stakes and fires and gets itself unstuck.
[*][b]Home life:[/b] it takes a free bed and empty chests, works towards better gear (gathering, smelting, crafting and upgrading at your stations), hunts, cooks, keeps its fires burning and repairs its gear and your base. It never chops, mines or breaks rocks inside a base: it heads out past it for wood and stone. While you are away it catches up on all of it.
[*][b]Farming:[/b] send it on the Plant crops or Harvest crops errand. With a cultivator it sows seeds in neat rows on the tilled ground at home (matching what already grows there); harvesting needs no tool. It fetches the cultivator and seeds from its own chests, and while you are away it brings in ripe crops and sows the free ground on its own.
[*][b]Needs like a player:[/b] food, stamina, skills that rise, carry weight, weather and rest.
[*][b]Gear slots of its own[/b], and its bag shows below your inventory: drag items between you, or give and take from its menu.
[*][b]A journal[/b] of its life, small talk that fits the moment, and a menu with its status, stats, orders, home, inventory, looks and companions.
[*][b]EpicMMO System:[/b] it earns experience and levels from what it kills and gathers.
[*][b]StarLevelSystem:[/b] it leaves companions alone, as it does players: no copies, no star levels or modifiers, and it never deletes one.
[*][b]Body Replacer (optional):[/b] with Magic Mike's Body Replacer installed, its looks menu also offers that mod's Einherjar bodies, game hairstyles and tattoos.
[*][b]Languages:[/b] English, Russian, German, Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, Polish and Turkish, following the game's language setting.
[/list]


[size=5][b]Configuration[/b][/size]

Settings are in [b]BepInEx/config/MagicMike.VillagePlebs.cfg[/b]. Change them in game in the [b]Mod Settings[/b] panel ([b]F7[/b]), or in r2modman/Gale/[url=https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Azumatt/Official_BepInEx_ConfigurationManager/]Configuration Manager[/url]. Fight Range and Show Decisions are also in the companion's menu. The settings marked synced are the server's in multiplayer.

[b]General[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Menu Key[/b] (default J)
[*][b]Command Key[/b] (default H)
[*][b]Assign Key[/b] (default Y)
[/list]

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Everyone in the world needs the mod, [b]the host too[/b]: without it the game deletes saved companions. Each companion belongs to whoever summoned it. The host decides how many companions each player can have, what they do while you're away, respawn time, base health and stamina, when they eat, starting skill and EpicMMO experience.

Restart the game after installing or updating so the companion registers cleanly.
